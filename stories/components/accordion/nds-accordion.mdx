## Group Accordion

Use `nds-accordion-group` to control open behavior:

- `mode="single"`: only one accordion stays open at a time.
- `mode="multi"`: multiple accordions can stay open together.

<Canvas of={AccordionStories.Group} />
<Controls of={AccordionStories.Group} />

<Source
  language="html"
  code={`<nds-accordion-group mode="single">
  <nds-accordion title="This is panel header" open>This is a placeholder text.</nds-accordion>
  <nds-accordion title="This is panel header">This is a placeholder text.</nds-accordion>
  <nds-accordion title="This is panel header">This is a placeholder text.</nds-accordion>
</nds-accordion-group>`}
/>

## Do

- Use concise header text that clearly describes the hidden content.
- Keep body content readable and split into short paragraphs.
- Prefer medium size by default; use small/large only where needed.
- Use group mode `single` for FAQ-like layouts.

## Don't

- Do not hide critical information users always need to see.
- Do not over-nest accordion sections.
- Do not use long vague titles.
- Do not mix single and multi-open behavior in the same group.
